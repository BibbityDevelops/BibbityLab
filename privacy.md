# Privacy Policy

**BibbityLab** · Last updated: 07-11-2026

BibbityLab is a photo editing app for iPad and iPhone, developed as a hobby project
by Raphael Hoheisel ("I", "me").

## Summary

BibbityLab does not collect, store or share any personal data. It has no account,
no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it makes no network connections.
Your photos and edits stay on your device and in the folders you choose.

## Data the app does not collect

BibbityLab does not collect, transmit or sell any data. In particular, it does not
collect:

- photos, photo metadata (such as location or camera details) or edits
- names, email addresses or other contact information
- usage data, device identifiers or location

The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code.

## Data stored on your device

To work, the app stores the following on your device only:

- **Edits, labels and presets** in the app's own storage.
- **Thumbnails** of your photos in a cache, to show the library quickly.
- **Optional backups of your edits** in a hidden folder named `.bibbitylab` inside
  the folders you added. You can turn this off in Settings › Backups.
- **Copies of photos you import** from the Photos app, in the folder "Imported from
  Photos".

This data never leaves your device through BibbityLab. It may be included in your
device's own iCloud or computer backup, depending on your settings. Copies of
imported photos are excluded from these backups. If folders you added are stored in
iCloud Drive or another cloud service, that service syncs them, including the backup
files, under its own privacy policy.

Deleting the app removes the data in its own storage. Backup files and photos in
folders outside the app (for example on an SD card or in iCloud Drive) stay where
they are until you delete them.

## Access to your photos and files

- **Folders:** BibbityLab can only access folders you choose in the Files picker.
- **Photos library:** importing uses Apple's photo picker. BibbityLab only receives
  the photos you select and has no access to the rest of your library.
- **Saving to Photos:** when you export an edited photo to the Photos app,
  BibbityLab asks for permission to add photos. It cannot read your library with
  this permission.

## Crash reports

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device settings
(Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements), Apple may provide me
with anonymous crash reports. These are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy
policy and do not contain your photos or personal information.

## Contact and support

If you write to me by email or on GitHub, I only use your message to answer it.
GitHub issues are public, so please don't include personal information there.

## Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an
updated date.
